## Tone principles for concise internal comms

Apply these on top of the 3P structure. When the work doesn't fit 3P cleanly,
these principles still govern the writing.

- Be clear and concise — cut every word that isn't load-bearing.
- Use active voice ("Fixed X", "Shipped Y"), not passive.
- Put the most important information first.
- Include relevant links and references (PRs, job names, tickets, dashboards).
- Match a terse, matter-of-fact status-report style — no throat-clearing, no
  significance inflation, no editorializing.
