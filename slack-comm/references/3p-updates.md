## What a 3P update is

3P stands for **Progress, Plans, Problems**. The audience is executives,
leadership, and teammates who have *some* context but not a lot. It must be
very succinct and to-the-point: readable in 30–60 seconds or less.

3Ps can cover a team of any size. The bigger the team, the less granular the
items. A single engineer's session recap sits at the granular end: concrete,
shipped, specific.

Three sections:

1. **Progress** — what was accomplished. Things shipped, milestones hit, bugs
   fixed, investigations closed. Data-driven, metrics where possible.
2. **Plans** — what's next / top-of-mind: follow-ups, monitoring, remaining
   high-priority work.
3. **Problems** — anything slowing things down: blockers, bugs, failed
   dependencies, missing resources.

## Formatting (strict — never deviate)

Pick an emoji that captures the vibe of the work and update.

```
[emoji] [Team Name] ([dates covered])
Progress: [1–3 sentences]
Plans: [1–3 sentences]
Problems: [1–3 sentences]
```

Each section: no more than 1–3 sentences — clear, to the point, data-driven,
metrics where possible. Tone is **matter-of-fact**, not prose-heavy. If a
section is empty, write `None`.
